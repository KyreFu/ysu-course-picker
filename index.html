Reading the graph

Solid arrows are hard prerequisites. Dashed arrows are corequisites — the schedule allows them concurrently. A filled node is one you've marked complete; a ringed node is one every prerequisite for which is satisfied. Alt-click a node — Option on a Mac — to mark it complete or put it back without leaving the graph. Selecting a course dims everything except its own chain: what it needs behind it, what it unlocks ahead. The view zooms in far enough to stay readable, so long chains will run off-screen — drag to follow them, or click empty space to zoom back out to the whole graph.

Caveats

EX210 and EX220 are merged into one node — same prerequisites, taken the same term. CM201 also lists CM100 Chinese Medical Terminology, not offered this term and not graphed. EX300 accepts WM360 or WM361; only WM361 runs in Fall 2026. Prerequisites can only be waived by approved Override Petition — check with the registrar before relying on this.
